Frequently Asked Questions

Where is your workshop located?

We are centrally located at UI Park, Lakoto Junction, Ajibode/Apete, Ibadan. It is easily accessible, and you can use the Google Maps link in the contact section for direct navigation to our doorstep.

What are your working hours?

Our workshop is open from Monday to Saturday, between 8:00 AM and 6:00 PM. We are closed on Sundays. For emergencies, you can still reach us via our WhatsApp line.

Do you install trackers for all car brands?

Yes, we install and configure high-quality GPS trackers for all vehicle models, including Toyota, Honda, Lexus, Mercedes, and more. Our trackers are reliable and easy to monitor via your smartphone.

Can I install a reverse camera if my car didn't come with a screen?

Absolutely! We specialize in retrofitting. We can supply and install a modern Android screen or a standalone monitor in your car, and seamlessly integrate a high-definition reverse camera into the system.

How long does an engine conversion take?

The duration depends on the vehicle and the type of conversion required. Some standard conversions take a few days, while complex ones may take longer. We always provide a clear timeline after a physical inspection of your car.

Do you fix electrical issues on luxury cars?

Yes, we handle European, American, and Asian cars. We are equipped with modern diagnostic tools and scanners capable of reading and clearing complex fault codes in luxury vehicles without guesswork.

What is error code scanning?

Error code scanning is a diagnostic process where we plug a specialized computer into your car's OBD-II port. This allows us to read exact fault codes from the car's ECU, so we can pinpoint and fix the issue accurately without replacing unnecessary parts.

Do you sell car parts or only install them?

We do both. Through our General Merchandising service, we sell high-quality auto parts and accessories. We also handle the professional installation of any parts purchased from us or provided by you.

Do you offer remote start for push-to-start cars?

Yes, we can integrate remote start systems into modern push-to-start vehicles. This allows you to start your car and cool the AC before you even step inside, adding both convenience and comfort.

How do I pay for your services?

We accept secure bank transfers to our official Opay account. All payment details are clearly listed in the Payment section of this website. Please confirm the total amount with us before making a transfer.

Do you offer any warranty on your installations?

Yes, we stand by the quality of our work. We provide a standard warranty on our electrical work, screen installations, and trackers. Specific warranty terms are discussed upon completion of your service.
